Practice Overview

Michelle Sathekge Occupational Therapy provides high-standard clinical care focused on practical participation. Every intervention respects the client's individual agency, lived context, and unique priorities—avoiding one-size-fits-all programs or defining success purely by impairment measures.

We work collaboratively alongside clients, families, treating medical practitioners, employers, and occupational health professionals within clear professional scope boundaries.

Professional Governance Notice:

Our clinical practice provides occupational therapy input within our accredited professional scope. In corporate and industrial contexts, recommendations support functional participation and collaborate with treating occupational health and statutory safety teams.

Clinical Services

Clinical Rehabilitation

Comprehensive, person-centred assessment and therapy supporting functional recovery, cognitive independence, and emotional well-being.

  • Neuro-Rehabilitation

    Specialised intervention restoring motor function, cognition, mobility, and independent daily participation for neurological conditions:

    Stroke Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) Multiple Sclerosis (MS) Parkinson’s Disease

  • Physical Rehabilitation

    Restorative physical therapy, adaptive retraining, and joint protection for trauma, orthopedic conditions, and post-operative recovery:

    Hand Injuries Joint Replacement Bone Fractures Amputations

  • Mental Health Rehabilitation

    Individual & Group Therapy

    Evidence-based occupational therapy facilitating routine stabilization, emotional coping skills, executive function, and social participation:

    Bipolar Disorder Depression Anxiety Schizophrenia
Corporate & Industry

Vocational Rehabilitation & Corporate Services

Assessment-informed functional evaluation and structured return-to-work recommendations coordinated with occupational health partners.

  • Functional Capacity Evaluation (FCE)

    Evaluating physical, cognitive, and environmental capacities against the actual demands of specific job roles.

  • Work Hardening

    Structured, goal-oriented conditioning programs restoring physical tolerance, task endurance, and work readiness.

  • Return-to-Work Programs

    Developing graded return-to-work plans in close coordination with treating clinicians and occupational health teams.

  • Task, Ergonomics & Environmental Adaptation

    Practical adaptations to workstations, tool use, and physical workflows based on thorough functional assessment.

  • Employer Consultation & Education

    Objective occupational therapy perspectives and education for HR, safety, and rehabilitation case managers.
