How to Paste in Microsoft Outlook:

  1. Click the green "Copy Formatted Signature" button above.
  2. Open Outlook.
  3. Click File > Options > Mail > Signatures (or start a New Email and click Signature > Signatures...).
  4. Click New, type a name (e.g. Michelle Signature).
  5. Click inside the signature box and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  6. Under "Choose default signature", select it for New messages and Replies/forwards.
  7. Click OK.
